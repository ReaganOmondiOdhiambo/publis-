A few things to know

Good questions.
Simple answers.

Do I need a ticket?

In this fictional concept, general entry is free. Workshops have limited places and would use a separate booking process. This example does not take bookings.

Do I need any creative experience?

Not at all. The sessions are designed for curious beginners as well as people with a regular creative practice. Materials are included in the concept.

What about accessibility?

The proposed venue brief includes step-free entry, accessible toilets and a quiet space. These are fictional design requirements, not verified facilities. The contact form demonstrates where visitors can ask about access.

Can I bring a friend?

Yes. The concept welcomes solo visitors and groups alike. Come for one session or make a day of it.
